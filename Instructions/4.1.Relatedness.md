## Inbreeding

One measure of assessing the extent of inbreeding is through the use of ROHs (runs of homozygosity).

```bash
plink --vcf ../vcfs/G3modc_2017.recode.vcf --make-bed --no-sex --no-parents --double-id --no-pheno --allow-extra-chr --out G3mod2017_plink --chr-set 28

plink -bfile G3mod2017_plink --homozyg --allow-extra-chr --homozyg-kb 100 --chr-set 28
```
