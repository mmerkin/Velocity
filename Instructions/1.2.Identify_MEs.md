The great variation in the number of chromosomes between species can make comparisons difficult e.g. a region in chromosome 3 in one species may be on chromosome 27 in another. As such, Ilik has 
suggested to order chromosomes based on their ancestral linkage groups, named "Merian elements" (MEs). See ([Wright *et al*, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-024-02329-4)).

## Identification of MEs

Chromosomes can be painted using busco genes to match up their equivalent ME, noting that most species have had some fusions and the two *Lysandra* species have undergone numerous fissions.
